# Hail Formation Simulation

A browser-based 2D simulation of how hail forms inside a thunderstorm, presented as a series of slides:

1. **Lifting** – warm, moist air is carried upward by the updraft.
2. **Freezing & Accretion** – supercooled droplets freeze onto ice embryos and growing hailstones.
3. **Layering** – turbulence cycles stones through wet and dry growth regions, building layers.
4. **Falling Hail** – stones grow too heavy for the updraft to support and fall out.

## Running

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step or dependencies.
